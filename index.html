Crate Sort: Calm Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Crate Sort: Calm Puzzle does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and makes no network connections. Your progress is stored only on your device. The only way information leaves the app is when you choose Share play report in settings, which hands a plain-text summary of your level tallies to your device's share sheet; the app itself sends nothing anywhere.
