Crate Sort: Calm Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Baca dalam Bahasa Indonesia

In effect from 18 October 2026.

In short

  • Crate Sort has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email address or age.
  • To show ads and to see how the levels play, the game sends Google data about your phone and how you play: an advertising ID, an app ID, your IP address (which gives a rough location), and the result of each attempt at a level.
  • In Europe (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google first asks for your consent. Analytics runs only if you agree.
  • In Indonesia, ads are never personalised and analytics never uses your advertising ID.
  • The game is for players aged 13 and over.
  • To see or delete what we hold about you, email andrewlie.business@outlook.com. The game's Contact row fills in the ID we need.

Who we are

Crate Sort: Calm Puzzle is a sorting puzzle game for Android, made by Andrew Lie, an individual developer in Jakarta, Indonesia, who publishes on Google Play as aethmos_. Andrew decides how your personal data is used (he is its controller) and is also the person responsible for data protection. "We" and "us" in this policy mean Andrew.

Contact for every privacy question and request: andrewlie.business@outlook.com.

What leaves your phone, and who receives it

Your progress, settings, level tallies and purchases you own are stored on your phone. The daily-level reminder is scheduled on your phone too, and sending it sends nothing. Your advertising ID, your IP address and the app-instance ID below are personal data, and so is anything linked to them. This is what leaves the phone.

Google AdMob: ads

Google AdMob shows the ads between levels and the optional ads you press for. To choose, show and count an ad, and to stop ad fraud, AdMob receives:

  • your phone's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which Google works out an approximate location;
  • information about your phone and the app: model, Android version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads: which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • diagnostics, such as how long an ad took to load and whether it failed.

Google uses this data as an independent controller, under the Google Privacy Policy and Google's controller-to-controller data protection terms. How Google uses data from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. In Europe, the consent message also names the ad partners whose ads AdMob may show.

Google Firebase: analytics and game settings

Firebase Analytics shows us how the game is played, so that we can find levels that are too hard and tune the game's settings. Google runs Firebase on our behalf (as our processor), under the Firebase data processing terms. Firebase receives:

  • an app-instance ID: a random ID made when the game is installed. A new one is made if you reinstall.
  • your advertising ID, except in Indonesia, and in Europe only once you have agreed to analytics;
  • your IP address, from which Google works out your country, region and city;
  • information about your phone: model, Android version and language;
  • how you installed the game, for example which ad campaign led you to it;
  • Firebase's own events: first opening, sessions and time spent, app updates, ads shown, and purchases (the item and its price);
  • each attempt at a level: the level (or the daily level's day), how the attempt ended (cleared, restarted or left), how long it lasted, the number of taps, undos, undo refills and jams, which attempt at that level it was, and the app and level-set versions;
  • a few game events: refilling undos or pressing boost (and whether an ad played), the No Ads card being shown or tapped, a theme or tune being unlocked or chosen, opening the game from the reminder, and the revenue from each ad shown (its value, currency and format);
  • your settings: piece marking, cue set, theme, tune, whether you own No Ads, and whether the reminder is on.

At most once a day, the game also asks Firebase Remote Config for updated game settings. That request carries a Firebase installation ID, the app-instance ID, the app version, your settings listed above, and your phone's language, region and time zone. In Europe it is made only once you have agreed to analytics.

Each day, Firebase copies the analytics data into our own Google Cloud project (BigQuery), stored in Jakarta, where we read it.

Google Play: purchases

No Ads, themes and tunes are bought through Google Play, which handles the payment. We receive the order: what was bought, when, the price, an order number, and the buyer's country (for some sales also the city, state and postal code). We never see your name, card or payment details. Google Play uses its data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Only if you choose

  • Share play report opens your phone's share sheet with a plain-text report you can read first: your level tallies, furthest level, marking choice and the app versions. It goes only where you send it.
  • Contact opens your email app with a message to us that has your app-instance ID in it. We receive your email address and what you write.

What we never collect

Your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or anything you type in the game.

Ads and analytics by region

All ads, everywhere, are limited to content suitable for general audiences (Google's "G" rating).

  • Europe (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland): at first launch, Google's consent message asks how ads and analytics may use your data. If you agree, ads may be personalised as you chose. If you decline, Google shows limited ads, which are not personalised, and no analytics is sent. You can change your answer at any time under Privacy choices in settings. Google decides who is in Europe from your IP address.
  • Indonesia: ads are never personalised, Google's treatment for teenage audiences applies to every ad, and Firebase does not collect your advertising ID. The game treats the phone as in Indonesia if any one of these says so: the SIM card's country, the mobile network's country, a time zone set to Jakarta, Pontianak, Makassar or Jayapura, or the phone's region set to Indonesia.
  • Everywhere else: ads may be personalised using your advertising ID. You can stop this in your Android settings (see Your controls).

Your controls

  1. Privacy choices (Europe): settings › Privacy choices reopens the consent message so you can change your answer. It takes effect at once.
  2. Your Android advertising ID settings: under Settings › Privacy › Ads (on some phones, Settings › Google › Ads) you can delete or reset your advertising ID. Once it is deleted, ads are no longer personalised with it and neither AdMob nor Firebase receives it.
  3. The mailbox: andrewlie.business@outlook.com, through the Contact row in settings, for any request below.

Uninstalling the game stops everything it sends.

Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and how we use it, and give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • stop using it, delete it, or restrict how we use it;
  • explain the legitimate interest we rely on, and stop processing based on it if you object;
  • withdraw your consent (in Europe, through Privacy choices);
  • where Indonesian law gives you the right, claim compensation for a breach of these rules.

How. Email us from settings › Contact, which puts your app-instance ID in the message. We cannot tell who a player is without it. With it, we can find, copy or delete everything Firebase and our BigQuery project hold under that ID. Requests are free. We confirm and act on them within 3×24 hours (72 hours), and if something takes longer, such as preparing a copy, we tell you when it will be ready.

Data Google holds as a controller (AdMob, Google Play) is not visible to us. Google's own tools reach it: delete or reset your advertising ID as above, and see My Ad Center and the Google Privacy Policy.

Complaints. Please write to us first. You may also complain to a regulator: in Indonesia, the personal data protection agency once it is formed and, until then, the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi); in Europe, the data protection authority where you live.

How long data is kept, and how

  • Firebase Analytics: data tied to your app-instance ID or advertising ID is kept for 2 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Our BigQuery project: each day's copy of the analytics data is deleted after 60 days. We keep summaries built from it, such as how many players cleared each level in a week. They carry no ID of any player and cannot be linked to you.
  • AdMob: as set out in Google's data retention policy.
  • Purchases: Google Play keeps your order history. We keep order records for 10 years, as Indonesian tax law requires.
  • Emails: kept until your question or request is settled, then deleted within 12 months. For a deletion request we keep a short record of what was deleted and when, without the deleted data.
  • On your phone: until you clear the game's data or uninstall it.

Security

Everything the game sends travels encrypted (HTTPS). The Firebase, Google Cloud, AdMob and Google Play Console accounts can be opened only by Andrew's Google account, protected by 2-step verification, and so is the mailbox. Raw analytics data expires after 60 days, and the summaries we keep carry no player ID. If a breach of personal data ever happens, we will notify the regulator, post a notice here for players and email anyone who has written to us, within 3×24 hours, as Indonesian law requires.

Transfers outside Indonesia

Andrew works from Indonesia. Google stores and processes the data above on its servers in the United States and other countries; our BigQuery copy is stored in Jakarta. As Indonesian law asks, before your data is transferred we tell you:

  • Purpose: to show ads, measure and tune the game, and deliver purchases.
  • Protection: Google's binding contract terms, which are its controller-to-controller terms for AdMob and its data processing terms for Firebase, and which include standard contractual clauses for data from Europe.
  • How you keep your rights: through our mailbox, and through Google's own tools for the data it holds. Google's terms oblige it to help us answer your requests.
  • Risks and how we reduce them: the protection in another country may differ from Indonesia's, and foreign authorities may be able to demand data from Google. We send no name, email address or precise location; no advertising ID reaches Firebase from Indonesia; player-level data is kept for only 2 months; and everything travels encrypted.

Children

Crate Sort is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not in Google Play's programme for families. Under Indonesian law anyone under 18 is a child. Because the game never asks your age, every player in Indonesia gets the protections described under Indonesia. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's data deleted, email us with the app-instance ID from the child's phone (settings › Contact).

Play-testing

Sign-ups to play-test go to a Google Form and are used only to send you a test link. Earlier browser test builds sent how a tester played, and any feedback they typed, under the tester code in their link to a Google Sheet that only Andrew can open. That play data is deleted 13 months after we received it; per-level totals are kept without the code. To have yours deleted sooner, email us with your tester code. Current test builds send what the game sends, as described above.

Changes to this policy

We post any change on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect, with the date it takes effect. A change in what the game sends ships only in an app version released after that, and that version's release notes on Google Play say so. Every earlier version of this page is kept in its public change history.

  • ID instance aplikasi: ID acak yang dibuat saat game dipasang. ID baru dibuat jika Anda memasang ulang.
  • ID iklan Anda, kecuali di Indonesia, dan di Eropa hanya setelah Anda menyetujui analitik;
  • alamat IP Anda, yang dipakai Google untuk mengetahui negara, wilayah, dan kota Anda;
  • informasi tentang ponsel Anda: model, versi Android, dan bahasa;
  • cara Anda memasang game, misalnya kampanye iklan mana yang membawa Anda ke game ini;
  • peristiwa bawaan Firebase: pembukaan pertama, sesi dan lama bermain, pembaruan aplikasi, iklan yang ditampilkan, dan pembelian (barang dan harganya);
  • setiap percobaan di sebuah level: levelnya (atau hari level harian), cara percobaan berakhir (selesai, diulang, atau ditinggalkan), lamanya, jumlah ketukan, undo, isi ulang undo, dan macet, percobaan keberapa di level itu, serta versi aplikasi dan versi set level;
  • beberapa peristiwa game: mengisi ulang undo atau menekan boost (dan apakah iklan diputar), kartu No Ads ditampilkan atau diketuk, tema atau lagu dibuka atau dipilih, membuka game dari pengingat, dan pendapatan dari setiap iklan yang ditampilkan (nilai, mata uang, dan formatnya);
  • pengaturan Anda: penanda keping, set isyarat suara, tema, lagu, apakah Anda memiliki No Ads, dan apakah pengingat aktif.

Paling sering sekali sehari, game juga meminta pengaturan game terbaru dari Firebase Remote Config. Permintaan itu membawa ID instalasi Firebase, ID instance aplikasi, versi aplikasi, pengaturan Anda yang disebut di atas, serta bahasa, wilayah, dan zona waktu ponsel Anda. Kami tidak mengirim nama, alamat email, atau lokasi persis; tidak ada ID iklan yang sampai ke Firebase dari Indonesia; data tingkat pemain hanya disimpan 2 bulan; dan semuanya dikirim terenkripsi.

Anak

Crate Sort dibuat untuk pemain berusia 13 tahun ke atas. Game ini tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun dan tidak termasuk dalam program keluarga Google Play. Menurut hukum Indonesia, setiap orang yang berusia di bawah 18 tahun adalah anak. Karena game tidak pernah menanyakan usia, setiap pemain di Indonesia mendapat pelindungan yang dijelaskan di bagian Indonesia. Jika Anda orang tua atau wali dan ingin data anak Anda dihapus, kirim email kepada kami dengan ID instance aplikasi dari ponsel anak (pengaturan › Contact).

Uji coba

Pendaftaran uji coba masuk ke Google Form dan hanya dipakai untuk mengirimkan tautan uji coba kepada Anda. Versi uji coba lama di browser mengirim cara penguji bermain, dan masukan yang mereka ketik, dengan kode penguji di tautan mereka ke Google Sheet yang hanya dapat dibuka Andrew. Data permainan itu dihapus 13 bulan setelah kami menerimanya; jumlah per level disimpan tanpa kode. Untuk menghapus data Anda lebih cepat, kirim email kepada kami dengan kode penguji Anda. Versi uji coba saat ini mengirim hal yang sama dengan game, seperti dijelaskan di atas.
