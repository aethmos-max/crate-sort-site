Crate Sort: Calm Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Crate Sort: Calm Puzzle is made by Andrew Lie, an individual developer in Indonesia, publishing on Google Play as aethmos_. Questions or requests: andrewlie.business@outlook.com

The app has no accounts and no ads, and it asks for no personal details. Your progress, settings and level tallies are stored only on your phone. Two things can leave the app: anonymous attempt summaries, if the recording switch is on, and a play report, if you choose to share one.

Anonymous play stats (the recording switch)

While the recording switch in settings is on, the app sends one short attempt summary each time you finish a try at a level. A summary contains only:

  • the level number;
  • how the try ended: cleared, restarted, or left (the app was closed or put in the background);
  • how long the try lasted;
  • how many taps, undos and jams the try had;
  • which try at that level it was (first, second, and so on);
  • the app version and level-set version.

A summary carries no identifier: no install ID, account, advertising ID, device model or other device data, and no timestamp beyond the time the server receives it. Summaries cannot be linked to each other or to you. They are used only to see where levels are too hard or too easy and to tune them.

Summaries are sent to a Google Sheet, through a Google Apps Script web app, that only the developer can open. Google stores this data on the developer's behalf. Summaries that can't be sent right away (for example, with no connection) wait on your phone and are sent later; if more than about 200 are waiting, the oldest are dropped. Sending never delays play.

Default. If your phone's time zone or region is in the European Economic Area (EEA), or the app can't tell where your phone is, the switch starts off, and nothing is sent unless you turn it on. Turning it on is your consent. Everywhere else (including the UK and Switzerland), the switch starts on.

Turning it off. Open settings (the gear icon) and turn off the recording switch. Sending stops at once and any summaries still waiting on your phone are deleted. Because summaries carry no identifier, ones already received can't be picked out as yours to delete.

Share play report

Choosing Share play report in settings opens your phone's share sheet with a plain-text report you can read before sending: your level tallies (clears, restarts, jams and undos per level), your furthest level, your marking choice, and the app and level-set versions. It goes only where you choose to send it.

Play-test builds

Want to try an early build? Sign up to play-test. Sign-ups go to a Google Form and are used only to send you a test link.

A play-test link opens an early build of the game in your phone's browser. That test build sends us how you play: for each try at a level, which stack you tapped and when, each undo, and whether you cleared the level, restarted it or left it. It is sent under the tester code in your link and a random ID the page keeps on your phone, never your name, to a Google Sheet that only the developer can open, and it is used only to tune the game's levels. If you send feedback from a test build, what you type is sent with your tester code to the same Google Sheet and used only to improve the game. A test link without a tester code sends nothing. To have your play data deleted, email the address above with your tester code.

Changes

If what the app sends changes, this page will be updated before an app version that sends it is released.
